Browsing this portfolio

This portfolio does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts or marketing cookies. Fonts and site assets are served with the site. The marketing page shows video thumbnails from YouTube, and plays videos through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) player only when you press play. It also loads Instagram post embeds near the Boatify case study, and Instagram may set its own cookies when those embeds load. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information to deliver pages and protect the service.

Contacting me

The contact form prepares an email draft in your browser. It does not submit your entries to a form service or save them in a portfolio database. If you send the email, your chosen email provider and mine process that message.

Other websites

Product sites, games and social profiles have their own privacy practices. Following a link takes you to that service.
